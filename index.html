mPod

The iPod went on sale on Nov. 10, 2001, promising a thousand songs in your pocket and very little else. The first release of mPod arrives on Nov. 10, 2026, 25 years to the day, and shares that modesty. It does one job on one device: It plays your music. There are no feeds, no advertisements and no notifications. The songs live in folders on your device, play without an internet connection and never leave it.

Slashdot offered its assessment on Oct. 23, 2001, the day Apple unveiled the iPod: “No wireless. Less space than a nomad. Lame.” It was a sharp line, and not an unreasonable one for its moment. A quarter-century on, it reads rather differently. We have kept the part that endured.

How to use it
  • To add music, choose Add Folder on the device. mPod reads the folder where it is and makes no copy.
  • Turn the wheel to move through a list. Press the centre button to choose.
  • Menu goes back one step.
  • On a keyboard, the arrow keys move, Enter chooses and Escape goes back.
